WHERE THE COPY COSTS TIME. stage(L+1) is issued after apply_tiered for layer L
and joined at L+1's MoE, so the copy is in flight during the post-MoE
all-reduce and during L+1's attention/indexer/dense-mm -- but NOT during hot or
cold Marlin, which run after the join and before the next stage. So:

  * hot Marlin +1.0% is near-noise, as the ordering predicts. PHASE7's earlier
    "+3.2% hot from copy-stream contention" was both the wrong number (it came
    from the unmatched 7-chunk mean) and the wrong mechanism -- hot Marlin is
    the one role that cannot be contended.
  * sparse_decode_fwd +4.7% (+25.9 ms) IS the contention, and it is the real
    cost of the feature. It was not reported at all before.

Net: -12.4% wall while paying ~26 ms of attention contention to save 257 ms of
cold Marlin.
