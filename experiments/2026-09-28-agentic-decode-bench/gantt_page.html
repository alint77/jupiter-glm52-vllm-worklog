DFlash2 Step Timeline

Two consecutive decode steps of GLM-5.3 W4A16 with the DFlash2 drafter (7 tokens, eager), DCP4, one user, on an agentic coding request. Every GPU kernel on all four GPUs, rank 0's CPU launch calls, and every idle gap on rank 0's GPU. Steps picked at the window's median period.

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, hover a bar for the kernel

Where the step goes. About 25 ms of each ~27 ms step is the verify pass: one CUDA graph of ~3,400 kernels on the main stream, with DCP's one-shot collectives on a side stream. Then ~0.5 ms of sampling, then the drafter: 188 eager kernels in ~1.3 ms.

Where the idle is. Almost none of it is in visible holes. It is ~5,400 bubbles per two steps, nearly all under 1 µs, at the boundary between one kernel and the next inside the graphs. Zoom to 100 µs to see them. None of the idle in these two steps is host-starved (the CPU had always issued the next kernel before the GPU went idle), so a CUDA graph for the drafter would not remove it; fewer, fused kernels would.

Reading the MoE bars. The MoE's five kernels per layer are chained with programmatic dependent launch: each starts early and waits on its predecessor, so their bars overlap and look longer than the work they do.
